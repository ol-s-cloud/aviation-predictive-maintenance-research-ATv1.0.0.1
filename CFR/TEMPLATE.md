## 1. Executive Summary

**Purpose:** Provide a concise overview of the report, highlighting key findings, potential market impact, and the main conclusions drawn from your analysis.

**Length:** No more than one page

**Key Elements:**
- Brief technology description
- Market opportunity summary
- Key competitive advantages
- Main risks and mitigation strategies
- Financial highlights (if applicable)
- Primary recommendations

**Audience:** Busy stakeholders who need quick, accessible insights

---

## 2. Introduction to the Technology

**Purpose:** Outline the fundamental concepts and functionalities of the technology, setting the stage for detailed exploration in subsequent sections.

**Length:** No more than one or two pages (brief)

**Key Elements:**
- What the technology does
- Core functionalities
- Key innovations
- Problem it solves
- Target applications

**Note:** Should we ask for more information about the tech, otherwise the description from us will not be precise enough to support this part.

---

## 3. Technology Readiness Level

**Purpose:** Evaluate the maturity level of the technology using the TRL framework.

**Key Elements:**
- Current TRL assessment (1-9 scale)
- Evidence supporting the TRL rating
- Progression path to higher TRLs
- Key milestones achieved
- Remaining technical challenges

**Reference:** NASA TRL framework or equivalent industry standard

---

## 4. Prototype Development

**Purpose:** Detail the status of any prototype development and results from initial tests.

**Key Elements:**
- Prototype development stage
- Testing results and data
- Performance metrics
- Validation outcomes
- Lessons learned
- Next iteration plans

---

## 5. Gap Analysis Towards Minimum Viable Product (MVP)

**Purpose:** Identify and discuss the differences between the current state of the technology and the requirements for a viable product that can enter the market.

**Key Elements:**
- Current technology state
- MVP requirements and specifications
- Technical gaps
- Resource gaps (talent, equipment, funding)
- Timeline gaps
- Steps needed to bridge gaps

**Goal:** Help pinpoint the critical steps needed to transform the concept into a market-ready product.

---

## 6. Market, Industry, and Application Overview

**Purpose:** Provide a comprehensive analysis of the market landscape where the technology will be introduced.

**Key Elements:**
- Market size and growth projections
- Industry trends and dynamics
- Potential application areas
- Market segmentation
- Customer needs and pain points
- Market drivers and barriers

**Goal:** Set the context for evaluating the technology's market fit and potential adoption.

---

## 7. Competitor Landscape

**Purpose:** Assess who are the existing and potential competitors within the market, providing insights into their strengths, weaknesses, and market strategies.

**Key Elements:**
- Direct and indirect competitors
- Competitor strengths and weaknesses
- Market positioning
- Competitive strategies

**Analysis Tools:**
- SWOT analysis
- Porter's Five Forces
- Competitive benchmarking

**Goal:** Analyse competitor positions and differentiate your technology effectively.

---

## 8. Commercial Advantages

**Purpose:** Highlight the unique benefits and selling points of your technology that can offer competitive edges over existing solutions.

**Key Elements:**
- Distinctive features
- Cost efficiencies
- Performance enhancements
- Unique value proposition
- Barriers to entry for competitors
- Sustainable competitive advantages

---

## 9. Market Research and Pricing Analysis

**Purpose:** Evaluate customer demand and pricing strategies based on comprehensive market data.

**Key Elements:**
- Market research methodology
- Customer demand analysis
- Pricing models evaluated
- Price sensitivity analysis
- Competitive pricing comparison
- Recommended pricing strategy

**Supporting Data:**
- Survey results
- Focus group findings
- Industry reports

**Goal:** Justify pricing decisions and demonstrate the product's market feasibility.

---

## 10. Customer Discovery and Validation

**Purpose:** Include detailed interactions with potential customers to validate the market need.

**Key Elements:**
- Customer discovery process
- Interview findings
- Survey results
- Focus group feedback
- Pilot testing data
- Customer validation metrics
- Key insights and learnings

**Note:** If there is a lot of data, summarise it in the report and put the data being referred to in an appendix.

---

## 11. Intellectual Property Strategy

**Purpose:** Outline the plan to safeguard the technology's IP rights.

**Key Elements:**
- Current IP status (patents, trademarks, trade secrets)
- Patent search results
- Freedom to operate analysis
- IP protection strategy
- Licensing considerations
- IP risks and mitigation

**Considerations:**
- Patents
- Trademarks
- Trade secrets
- Copyrights (if applicable)

---

## 12. Regulatory and Legal (Optional)

**Purpose:** Discuss any regulatory, compliance, or legal issues that could influence the progression of your product to market.

**Key Elements:**
- Relevant regulations and standards
- Certification requirements
- Compliance pathways
- Legal considerations
- Regulatory timeline
- Regulatory risks

**Note:** This section is optional but highly relevant for aviation technology.

---

## 13. Risk Analysis

**Purpose:** Provide an analysis of potential risks across technical, market, financial, and regulatory domains.

**Key Elements:**
- Technical risks
- Market risks
- Financial risks
- Regulatory risks
- Operational risks
- Mitigation strategies for each risk
- Risk prioritization matrix

**Framework:** Risk assessment matrix (likelihood vs. impact)

---

## 14. Potential Business Models

**Purpose:** Write an overview of the potential ways the technology could generate revenue.

**Key Elements:**
- Business model options (SaaS, licensing, direct sales, etc.)
- Target market for each model
- Value proposition
- Revenue streams
- Cost structure
- Scalability potential
- Strengths and weaknesses of each model

**Goal:** Determine the most viable option for commercialisation.

**Considerations:**
- Target market
- Value proposition
- Scalability

---

## 15. Scalability Analysis

**Purpose:** Discuss the scalability potential of the technology and any associated challenges.

**Key Elements:**
- Technical scalability
- Operational scalability
- Market scalability
- Supply chain considerations
- Resource requirements for scaling
- Macroeconomic factors
- Scaling risks and challenges

---

## 16. Financial Overview

**Purpose:** Provide a summary of the projected costs, revenues, and financial projections associated with bringing the product to market.

**Key Elements:**
- Development costs
- Operating costs
- Revenue projections
- Break-even analysis
- Funding requirements
- Return on investment estimates
- Financial assumptions

**Note:** If applicable. Include only if financial data is available.

---

## 17. Timeline for Development

**Purpose:** Outline critical development milestones and estimated timelines for reaching each stage.

**Key Elements:**
- Major milestones
- Timeline for each milestone
- Dependencies and critical path
- Resource allocation timeline
- Go-to-market timeline

**Format:** Gantt chart or milestone roadmap recommended

---

## 18. Stakeholder Analysis

**Purpose:** Identify and analyse key stakeholders' interests, influences, and potential impacts on bringing the product to market.

**Key Elements:**
- Stakeholder identification
- Stakeholder interests and motivations
- Power and influence mapping
- Engagement strategies
- Potential conflicts
- Alignment opportunities

**Framework:** Power/Interest grid or Stakeholder mapping matrix

---

## 19. Sustainability Considerations

**Purpose:** Analyse the sustainability aspects of the technology, including environmental impacts and lifecycle considerations.

**Key Elements:**
- Environmental impact assessment
- Resource efficiency
- Lifecycle analysis
- Carbon footprint
- Circular economy considerations
- Sustainability benefits
- Long-term environmental implications

---

## 20. Non-Financial and Ethical Review

**Purpose:** Address ESG considerations, ethical issues and societal implications of the use of the technology in the market.

**Key Elements:**
- ESG (Environmental, Social, Governance) considerations
- Ethical implications
- Social impact
- Data privacy and security
- Safety considerations
- Societal benefits and concerns
- Responsible innovation practices

---

## 21. Partnership and Collaboration Opportunities

**Purpose:** Highlight potential partnerships or collaborations that could support the technology's development and commercialisation.

**Key Elements:**
- Potential partners (companies, institutions, organizations)
- Strategic value of partnerships
- Collaboration models
- Partnership requirements
- Mutual benefits
- Risk sharing opportunities

**Categories:**
- Technology partners
- Industry partners
- Academic collaborations
- Distribution partners
- Investment partners

---

## 22. Exit Strategies

**Purpose:** Discuss potential exit strategies for investors or developers.

**Key Elements:**
- Acquisition potential
- IPO possibilities
- Licensing agreements
- Strategic partnerships
- Timeline for exits
- Valuation considerations
- Industry precedents

---

## 23. Summary of Commercial Potential

**Purpose:** Succinctly outline the key findings from your analysis, highlighting the market opportunity, competitive advantage, and potential for success.

**Key Elements:**
- Market opportunity recap
- Competitive positioning summary
- Key success factors
- Critical challenges
- Overall commercial viability assessment

**Note:** This is a synthesis section, pulling together insights from previous sections.

---

## 24. Conclusions and Recommendations

**Purpose:** Provide a concise (evidenced-based) summary of the key insights drawn from your analysis and offer actionable recommendations based on these findings to guide future decision-making and strategy development.

**Key Elements:**
- Key conclusions from analysis
- Go/No-Go recommendation (if applicable)
- Strategic recommendations
- Next steps
- Priority actions
- Resource requirements
- Timeline for implementation

**Note:** Recommendations should be specific, actionable, and prioritized.
